# Peyk

**Too much inbound. Not enough attention to spend on it.**

Peyk is an umbrella brand over the two legs of corporate communication: the
**email channel** and the **social channel**. Two independent products, each
with its own contract and its own KVKK responsibility. Customer data is not
shared between them.

## Peyk Inbox — email operations

> Kurumsal mail trafiği için AI destekli vaka yönetimi

Turns a corporate mailbox into a case queue: classifies incoming mail,
opens and assigns cases, and tracks SLA in the open. Built for operations
teams that live in a shared inbox and need to know what is late before the
customer tells them.

Available as SaaS or on-premises.

## Peyk Signal — social operations

> Sosyal medya trafiği için AI destekli odak filtresi
>
> *AI-powered social media attention filter*

Watches connected social accounts, reads every incoming message, scores it,
and raises an alert only for the ones that need a person. Everything else
waits or is ignored — deliberately, and on the record, so you can audit
what it decided not to wake you for.
